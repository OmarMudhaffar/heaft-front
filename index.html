Intelligence

The AI that lifts with you.

Your plan. Remembered.

Upload any program — PDF, spreadsheet, photo. HEAFT digitizes it, tracks every session, and builds a complete history. From day one, the AI knows exactly where you are.

Every weight, justified.

HEAFT knows your last PR, your bar speed trend, and your recovery. It prescribes exact loads — not ranges. A specific weight with a specific reason.

Adapts. Automatically.

Missed a session? Crushed your PR? HEAFT recalculates next week's load based on what actually happened — not what was scheduled. The plan is always current.

Process

Day one to done.

01

Tell HEAFT your baseline.

Enter your current lifts, upload your plan, or let the AI build one from your goals and body metrics. Setup takes under five minutes.

02

Follow today's session.

HEAFT tells you what to lift, in what order, with how much rest. Log each set with one tap. Rest timer starts automatically.

03

HEAFT adjusts. You show up.

After every session the AI updates your load for next week. No spreadsheets, no guesswork. Just show up and lift what it tells you.
